In search of Speed

A wonderful pixel-art RPG adventure across the wastelands of a distopic future where only the fastest can survive

Many places to explore

The refueling station, it may cost a little but "better moving than standing" they say. Surprisingly, they sell something to drink as well; but I would skip this cocktail.

The feeding station, a hungry traveler doesn't make it far, and you may also taste worse for the outside creatures. Watch out for that "Exotic Soup", something seems to be swimming in there.

The ice-cream station, it may be pointless and with a suspicious sour taste, but pour that cone left over in your gas tank and you'll be surprised!

The supplies station, the place you want to be when you have enough money. The clerk may be rude, but damn those bullets he sells pierce good!

This game makes your heart race from the beginning till the very end. An oustanding adventure where you'll be re-thinking your survival strategy over and over again, as the world changes under your speeding wheels.
